# Asset provenance

- Character portraits and perk icons: [Zawgalicious/DBDImages](https://github.com/Zawgalicious/DBDImages), imported as local, optimized WebP files under `public/assets/characters` and `public/assets/perks`.
- Character and perk metadata: community [DBD API](https://dbd.tricky.lol/api/characters) and [perk endpoint](https://dbd.tricky.lol/api/perks), fetched November 6, 2026. The exact import timestamp and image-tree identifier are recorded in `src/catalog.json`.
- This snapshot includes only entries with matching artwork: 89 characters and 305 perks. It is not a claim of a complete or up-to-date live roster. Perk values use the highest available tier in the source. Unresolved source placeholders are shown as an em dash.
- Game names, portraits, and icons belong to Behaviour Interactive and their respective owners. Community hosting does not transfer ownership. The application identifies itself as an unofficial fan project.
- Fonts: Manrope and Cormorant Garamond, bundled locally through their Fontsource packages under the SIL Open Font License.
- Interface icons: Lucide, distributed under the ISC license.
- Tarot card back: original art generated with the built-in image generation tool, optimized as `public/assets/tarot-back.webp`. The original is preserved in `design/tarot-back-original.png`; its generation prompt is in `design/tarot-back-prompt.txt`.
- Forest environment: original art generated with the built-in image generation tool, stored as `public/assets/forest-night.webp` and the smaller `public/assets/forest-mobile.webp`. The source is preserved in `design/forest-night-original.png`; the exact prompt is in `design/forest-night-prompt.txt`.
- Distressed tarot back: an edit of the original tarot back using the built-in image generation tool. The active texture is `public/assets/tarot-back-worn.webp`. The source and exact edit prompt are preserved in `design/tarot-back-worn-original.png` and `design/tarot-back-worn-prompt.txt`. The earlier artwork is retained.
- The organic Entity is original procedural Three.js geometry: tapered, branching roots, curling rootlets, knots, and bark fibers. Its bark bump texture is generated locally; no external model, image request, or additional graphics canvas is required. Foreground branches, spectral chains, ash, and ink reveals are original SVG/CSS components.
- Background theme: [Post Apocalyptic Wastelands / Horror Atmosphere](https://opengameart.org/content/horror-atmosphere) by Juhani Junkala (SubspaceAudio), CC0 1.0. The full loop-ready track is loudness adjusted and encoded locally as `public/assets/audio/wastelands-theme.mp3`.
- Card foley: [Playing Card Sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/playing-card-sounds) by Brian MacIntosh (BMacZero), CC0 1.0. Shuffle, cut, and contact recordings are filtered and faded.
- Root cracks, reveal impact, and perk seals: selected recordings from [35 Wooden Cracks/Hits/Destructions](https://opengameart.org/content/35-wooden-crackshitsdestructions) by Independent.nu (submitted by qubodup), CC0 1.0; noise reduced, trimmed, gently pitched, filtered, and faded. Quiet synthesized low resonances reinforce the heartbeat, impact, and seals.
- Heartbeat: [Heartbeat (single sound)](https://opengameart.org/content/heartbeat-single-sound) by Independent.nu (submitted by qubodup), CC0 1.0; filtered and faded.
- Entity breathing and veil rise: Moan 01 from [Ghost Monster Voice Moaning & Growling](https://opengameart.org/content/ghost-monster-voice-moaning-growling) by Iwan Gabovitch (qubodup), CC0 1.0; trimmed, slowed, filtered, faded, and reversed for the rise. These are creature sound recordings, not spoken dialogue.
- The background theme is the same locally bundled MP3. Ritual effects are delivered as 44.1 kHz, 16-bit PCM WAV to avoid additional lossy encoding after editing the source recordings. A damped stereo delay room provides reverb without a generated noise impulse. Warm EQ and gentle dynamics apply only to effects; the music path is unchanged. The exact source URLs, downloaded SHA-256 hashes, and editing recipes are recorded in `design/audio-sources.json`; human-readable credits ship in `public/assets/audio/CREDITS.txt`. No official Dead by Daylight audio is included.

The application makes no external artwork requests at runtime. The import script is a development tool; API or repository availability does not affect an existing build.
