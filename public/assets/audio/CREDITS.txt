Audio assets are locally bundled edits of CC0 1.0 sources.
The background track remains MP3; effects use 44.1 kHz / 16-bit PCM WAV.
CC0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Post Apocalyptic Wastelands (Loop Ready) - Juhani Junkala / SubspaceAudio
https://opengameart.org/content/horror-atmosphere
Used as the full looping background theme; loudness adjusted and encoded as MP3.

Playing Card Sounds - Brian MacIntosh / BMacZero
https://opengameart.org/content/playing-card-sounds
Shuffle, cut, and contact recordings; filtered and faded.

35 Wooden Cracks/Hits/Destructions - Independent.nu, submitted by qubodup
https://opengameart.org/content/35-wooden-crackshitsdestructions
Selected cracks and impacts; noise reduced, trimmed, gently pitched, filtered, and faded.

Heartbeat (single sound) - Independent.nu, submitted by qubodup
https://opengameart.org/content/heartbeat-single-sound
Noise-reduced, trimmed, and filtered heartbeat recording.

Ghost Monster Voice Moaning & Growling - Iwan Gabovitch / qubodup
https://opengameart.org/content/ghost-monster-voice-moaning-growling
Moan 01; noise reduced, pitch lowered, trimmed, filtered, faded, and reversed for the veil rise.

The effects engine adds quiet synthesized low resonances and a damped stereo delay room.
The background theme and its playback processing are unchanged.

Source URLs, SHA-256 hashes, and exact FFmpeg recipes: design/audio-sources.json.
No official Dead by Daylight music or sound recordings are used.
